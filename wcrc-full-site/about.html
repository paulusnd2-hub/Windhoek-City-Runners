About WCRC

“This is my City, I must run it.”

Windhoek City Runners Club is an active running club serving runners of all levels in Namibia. We organise social runs, challenges and races, support members in their training goals, and foster a welcoming community that puts people first.

This website is the Club’s main digital platform for membership, event registration, club attire and news — designed to reduce manual administration and make it easy for every runner to take part.

This page is part of an interactive prototype prepared in response to the WCRC Terms of Reference for a new website, membership system, event management system and online shop.
