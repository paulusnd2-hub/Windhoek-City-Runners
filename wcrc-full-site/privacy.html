Privacy Policy

How Windhoek City Runners Club handles your personal information.

Prototype placeholder: this page illustrates where a full privacy policy will live. The wording below is a draft outline only and must be reviewed by the club committee (and, if needed, legal counsel) before the site goes live.

Information we collect

Name, contact details, date of birth, ID/passport number, and — for junior members — parent/guardian details, collected via the membership and event registration forms for the purpose of managing membership, race entries and club communication.

How we use it

To process membership applications and renewals, register runners for events, fulfil shop orders, issue receipts, and send club communications (news, event reminders, newsletter) to members and subscribers who opt in.

Payment information

Card and payment details are processed by a licensed third-party payment gateway in the live system — WCRC does not store card numbers.

Sharing

Personal information is not sold. It may be shared with event sponsors or timing partners only where necessary to run an event (e.g. race results, medical emergency contacts), and with service providers who support hosting, payments and email delivery.

Your rights

Members can request to view, correct or delete their personal information by contacting the club at whkcityrunners@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to whkcityrunners@gmail.com.
