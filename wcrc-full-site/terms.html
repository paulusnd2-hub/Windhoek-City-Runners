Terms & Conditions

Membership, events and shop terms for Windhoek City Runners Club.

Prototype placeholder: this page illustrates where the club's full terms and conditions will live. The outline below must be reviewed and finalised with the club committee before the site goes live.

Membership

Membership is subject to approval by club officials and to the club constitution and code of conduct. Fees are payable annually and are non-refundable once membership is activated. Junior membership requires parent/guardian consent and an accepted indemnity.

Events

Runners take part in club events at their own risk. By registering, you confirm you are medically fit to participate. Entry fees are non-refundable but may be transferable at the club's discretion. Event details (distance, route, start time) may change and will be communicated where possible.

Online shop

Orders are subject to stock availability. Items are collected at a club venue or event unless delivery is arranged. Faulty items may be exchanged within a reasonable period — contact the club to arrange this.

Code of conduct

Members are expected to treat fellow runners, volunteers and the public with respect at all club activities. The committee may suspend or terminate membership for conduct that breaches this code.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to whkcityrunners@gmail.com.
